> Until estimating is on a sounder basis, individual managers will need to stiffen their backbones and defend their estimates with the assurance that their poor hunches are better than wish-derived estimates.  
—Frederick P. Brooks

[The Mythical Man Month](https://micro.blog/books/9780132119160) was written in 1974, and it still feels like despite our many new techniques, the software industry's estimation hasn't evolved much past "defend an educated hunch".
