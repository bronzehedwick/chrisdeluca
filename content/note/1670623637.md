TIL there's a movement to refer to Neovim/Vim as a [PDE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QMVIJhC9Veg), or Personalized Development Environment, instead of a text editor. "Text editor" can be confused with categorically different editors like Notepad++, while PDE indicates the deep personalization and power available.
